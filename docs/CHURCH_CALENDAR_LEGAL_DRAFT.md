# Church Calendar planning portal: proposed legal updates

**DRAFT FOR OWNER AND LEGAL REVIEW ONLY.** This is a discussion draft, not legal
advice or a published policy. Do not place it on the public Privacy or Terms
pages or enable the planner acceptance gate until the church owner and its legal
advisers approve the final language and its effective versions.

## Proposed Privacy Policy additions

> When a ministry participates in church calendar planning, we may collect the
> name, email address, telephone number, ministry affiliation, and planning
> access activity of the people authorized to prepare its plan. We may also
> collect proposed event information, descriptions intended for publication,
> internal planning notes, contact details, expected attendance, locations,
> online meeting links, requested rooms and resources, and submission and
> review history.
>
> Church administrators use this information to coordinate and review proposed
> events, communicate with planners, maintain an audit history, and administer
> access. Authorized planning contacts and church staff may see information
> needed for those purposes. Information explicitly designated for publication
> may later be shared through the church calendar if the church approves it.
> Providers that host, secure, or deliver the service may process information
> on the church's behalf. A room or resource request does not itself make the
> information public or guarantee availability.
>
> The church retains planning and audit information under its applicable
> records practices. Contact the church office for questions about a planning
> record or access to it.

## Proposed Terms of Service additions

> Planning invitations are intended for the named recipient. Keep invitation
> links private, use only access granted to you, and sign out on shared devices.
> Contact the church office if you believe your link was shared or compromised.
>
> Submit accurate information and only information you are authorized to share.
> Do not include sensitive personal information in event descriptions or notes
> unless the church has specifically requested it. Identify material intended
> for public display carefully. A proposed event, room, or resource is a request
> and is not confirmed until the church communicates approval.
>
> The church may request corrections, change the planning process, or restrict
> access when needed to administer the calendar.

## Decisions for legal review

- Confirm the appropriate basis and exact wording for per-planner acceptance;
  distinguish acknowledgment from any consent that can be withdrawn.
- Specify actual retention periods, requests for access/correction/deletion,
  responsible contact information, and applicable service-provider disclosures.
- Confirm whether public event contacts and online meeting URLs need additional
  publication warnings or approval, and whether minors' information is allowed.
- Approve final changes to `privacy.md` and `terms-of-service.md`, effective
  versions, and the corresponding public pages before enabling access.
