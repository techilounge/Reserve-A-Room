Some rooms may only be reserved within a specified period.

For example, a room may be configured so that reservations can only be made up to four weeks in advance.

Reserve-A-Room may prevent you from selecting dates beyond that period.

## **7\. Reservations That Require Approval**

Some rooms require approval from a Stonehill Admin or Super Admin.

When a room requires approval:

* Your request will initially be marked **Pending**  
* The requested time may be temporarily held while the request is pending  
* An administrator may review the reservation information  
* The request may be approved or declined  
* You will normally receive an email informing you of the decision

A pending reservation is **not an approved reservation**.

Do not assume you have permission to use the room until you receive approval.

---

## **8\. Reservations That Do Not Require Approval**

Some rooms may be configured for immediate reservation.

If:

* The room is available  
* Your reservation satisfies the applicable rules  
* No scheduling conflict exists  
* The reservation is successfully processed

the reservation may immediately receive **Approved** status without administrative review.

An automatically approved reservation remains subject to these Terms and applicable Stonehill facility policies.

---

## **9\. Reservation Statuses**

Reserve-A-Room may use statuses including:

### **Pending**

The reservation is waiting for administrative review.

### **Approved**

The reservation has been approved or was confirmed automatically because the room did not require prior approval.

### **Declined**

The reservation request was not approved.

### **Cancelled**

The reservation is no longer active.

Only reservations with an active Approved status authorize use of a room at the scheduled time.

## **10\. Scheduling Conflicts**

Reserve-A-Room is designed to prevent overlapping active reservations for the same room.

A room or time that appeared available while you were completing the reservation form may become unavailable before you submit it.

A reservation is not established until the Service successfully records it and displays a confirmation.

If a scheduling conflict occurs, you may need to choose another room or time.
